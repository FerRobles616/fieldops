FieldOps

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

FieldOps is a portfolio work-order application for field service teams. This policy explains what data the demo application handles, why it is used, and how to request deletion.

FieldOps is a public demo. The demo accounts and seed data are intentionally visible. Do not upload real customer records, personal information, or private photos.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: email address, display name, and role (technician or admin).
  • Work data: work orders, customer names and contact details, notes, schedules, and status changes that users enter.
  • Photos: images attached to work orders for documentation and PDF reports.
  • Device data: a local cache of work orders and photos so the app can work offline.
  • Server logs: standard request metadata such as IP address and timestamps for security and reliability.

How the information is used

  • To authenticate users and enforce technician/admin access rules.
  • To store and sync work orders, customer records, photos, and PDF reports.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the demo service.

FieldOps does not use advertising SDKs, does not sell personal data, and does not use the data for profiling or marketing.

Storage and service providers

The API and PostgreSQL database run on Render. The web demo and source code are hosted on GitHub. These providers process data on our behalf to run the service. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. The app stores the local offline cache on the device; uninstalling the app clears that local cache.

Retention

Demo data is kept while the demo environment is active. Work orders and photos may remain in the database until they are deleted or the demo is reset. Local device data remains until the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared.

Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2, authentication uses short-lived access tokens, and access is role-based. No system is perfectly secure; do not use the public demo for confidential data.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete work orders and documents through the app where the feature is available, clear the app's local storage by uninstalling it, or request deletion of demo records by opening an issue at github.com/FerRobles616/fieldops/issues. Include the email address used for the demo account and the records you want removed.

Children

FieldOps is a business tool and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may change as the demo evolves. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the GitHub issue tracker: FerRobles616/fieldops/issues.
